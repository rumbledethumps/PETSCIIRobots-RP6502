# Dying, and the screens that follow.

# plat_game_over has two branches and only one of them had ever run under test.
# On a death it faces the player's sprite FACE_DEAD, holds for 100 frames while
# BACKGROUND_TASKS keeps running, then shows the box, the endgame statistics and
# LOS_MSG; on a win it skips straight to the box. tests/emu/endgame.txt covers
# the win.
#
# Getting killed is harder than it sounds. The hazards a route can reach stop
# well short: level-e's rollerbot takes twelve health down to one and loses
# interest. level-k has an evilbot at 9,5, three squares from the player's
# start, and poking health down to 1 first makes the first hit fatal -- the game
# still kills him through its own INFLICT_DAMAGE, which is the path worth
# exercising.
#
# The route was recorded by
#     tools/playthrough.py k 9 5 --keys --poke "$776C $07" --poke "$79C0 $01"
# RAM addresses from build/.../robots.map: KEYS $776C, UNIT_HEALTH $79C0. The
# keys are poked because level-k's route passes a locked door; without them the
# driver walks around and never meets the evilbot.
